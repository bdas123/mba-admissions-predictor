See how your GMAT, GPA and profile shift your MBA odds

Move the sliders or paste your profile. Pick schools from the dropdown to build your list. Each school shows two distributions: your estimated chance of admission, and the scholarship you might receive if admitted. The highlighted bar and large number show the median.

This is an estimate learned from self-reported applicant outcomes, not an admissions decision. Essays, recommendations, interviews and fit drive real outcomes and aren’t modeled. The methodology page lists every assumption and limitation.

The interpreter reads your text with transparent pattern rules, entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server or an AI service.
